# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 26: Cloud Fundamentals

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **A.** In IaaS the customer manages and patches the guest operating system.
2. **A.** PaaS supplies a managed runtime or application platform.
3. **A.** Customers configure SaaS identity, sharing, data, retention, and tenant policy.
4. **A.** Management access can create, alter, or delete resources and controls at scale.
5. **A.** Managed identities provide short-lived, platform-controlled authentication without embedded secrets.
6. **A.** The service decrypts data for a principal that policy authorizes, including unintended public access.
7. **A.** Metadata can contain configuration and temporary workload credentials.
8. **A.** IaC makes infrastructure repeatable, versioned, testable, and reviewable.
9. **A.** Central logs can survive workload deletion and support cross-service correlation.
10. **A.** Access review is a people-driven operational process.
