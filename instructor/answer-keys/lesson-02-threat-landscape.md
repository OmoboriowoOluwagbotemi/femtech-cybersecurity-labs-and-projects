# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 2: Threat Landscape

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **B.** Ransomware commonly denies access through encryption and may also support data-theft extortion.
2. **B.** A nation-state actor is typically associated with strategic espionage, influence, or disruption objectives.
3. **B.** The Cyber Kill Chain describes high-level stages of an intrusion.
4. **B.** An ATT&CK tactic describes the adversary's tactical objective.
5. **C.** Early incident analysis should be based on observable evidence rather than unsupported attribution or destructive action.
6. **A.** A keylogger records keystrokes and may expose credentials or sensitive text.
7. **A.** Insider threats involve abuse or misuse of legitimate internal access.
8. **A.** Delivery is the Kill Chain stage where a malicious attachment, link, or payload reaches the target.
9. **A.** An ATT&CK technique describes the method an adversary uses to achieve a tactical goal.
10. **A.** Tested backups and recovery procedures reduce ransomware impact by supporting restoration.

Short answer and scenario responses should use evidence-based reasoning, connect threats to risk, and recommend practical controls.
