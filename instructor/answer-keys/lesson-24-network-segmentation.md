# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 24: Network Segmentation

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **A.** A VLAN forms a logical Layer 2 broadcast domain.
2. **A.** A trunk commonly transports frames for multiple VLANs using tags.
3. **A.** Routing and permissive policy can reconnect VLANs at Layer 3.
4. **A.** A DMZ places exposed services in a controlled intermediate zone.
5. **A.** Traditional extended ACLs evaluate source, destination, protocol, and port.
6. **A.** East-west describes communication among internal systems or workloads.
7. **A.** Microsegmentation applies fine-grained workload-level policy.
8. **A.** Negative tests demonstrate that unauthorized paths are actually blocked.
9. **A.** Two interfaces can unintentionally bridge or route around an enforcement point.
10. **A.** Rule review is a people-driven operational process.
