# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 8: Network and System Attacks

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **B.** Public job advertisements can reveal technology without directly probing the target.
2. **B.** Open means a service accepted or positively responded to the relevant probe; it does not prove compromise or vulnerability.
3. **A.** Enumeration uses service-specific interaction to collect details beyond host and port reachability.
4. **A.** ARP resolves addresses within a local Layer 2 broadcast domain and is not routed normally.
5. **A.** Poisoning can make a trusted name resolve to an unintended address.
6. **B.** Correct certificate validation can detect that the reached endpoint is not authorized for the requested HTTPS name.
7. **A.** DDoS distributes attack traffic or requests across multiple sources or infrastructure.
8. **B.** `arp` is the Wireshark display filter for ARP frames.
9. **A.** Authorized scanners and monitoring systems can resemble attacks, so authorization is an essential first correlation step.
10. **A.** A response procedure is performed through people and process, making it operational.
