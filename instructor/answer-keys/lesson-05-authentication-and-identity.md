# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 5: Authentication and Identity

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **B.** A password and security key combine knowledge and possession factors.
2. **B.** The identity provider authenticates identities and issues protected assertions or tokens.
3. **A.** OAuth 2.0 delegates authorization; OIDC adds an identity layer for authentication.
4. **A.** A salt makes identical passwords derive different stored values.
5. **B.** Password spraying tests a small set of likely passwords across many accounts.
6. **C.** FIDO2 is origin-bound and designed to resist credential phishing.
7. **B.** Token theft requires session revocation plus investigation of the endpoint and account activity.
8. **C.** Authorization determines permitted resources and actions after authentication.
9. **B.** A fast general-purpose hash allows high-rate offline guessing.
10. **C.** Service identities need accountable ownership and only the permissions required.

Scenario answers should connect identity evidence to business risk, containment, investigation, recovery, and control improvement.
