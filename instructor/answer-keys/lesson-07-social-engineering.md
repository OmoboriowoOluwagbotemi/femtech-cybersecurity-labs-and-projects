# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 7: Social Engineering

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **B.** Spear phishing is tailored to a selected identity, role, or organization.
2. **B.** BEC commonly seeks payment diversion, payroll change, or sensitive business data.
3. **A.** Vishing uses voice communication to manipulate the target.
4. **A.** `Reply-To` directs where a mail client's reply is sent.
5. **B.** A compromised legitimate account can send fully authenticated malicious mail.
6. **B.** Because newer relays prepend entries, analysts generally reconstruct the chain from the oldest relevant trusted entry upward.
7. **B.** Password change alone does not revoke stolen sessions or address endpoint compromise.
8. **A.** Pretexting builds a believable identity and scenario to justify a request.
9. **C.** Independent verification uses contact information already trusted by the organization.
10. **C.** A directive control instructs required verification behavior.

Scenario responses should address human, technical, and business evidence without blaming the reporter or affected user.
