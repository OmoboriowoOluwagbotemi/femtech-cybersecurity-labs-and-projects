# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 10: Virtualization Fundamentals

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **A.** The hypervisor presents and manages virtual hardware and schedules access to physical resources.
2. **B.** A Type 2 hypervisor runs as software on a general-purpose host operating system.
3. **B.** Bridged mode gives the guest direct presence on the physical LAN.
4. **B.** Host-only mode supports controlled guest-to-guest and host-to-guest communication without normal physical-LAN access.
5. **A.** NAT commonly permits outbound communication, so the guest is not disconnected or fully isolated.
6. **A.** A snapshot commonly depends on the VM's base and delta storage rather than being an independent recoverable copy.
7. **A.** Clones can duplicate hostnames, keys, certificates, identifiers, and embedded credentials.
8. **A.** Network mode and authorized scope determine whether lab traffic can reach unintended systems.
9. **A.** Management access can control consoles, storage, networking, and workload state.
10. **A.** Snapshot and recovery procedures are people-driven operational processes.
