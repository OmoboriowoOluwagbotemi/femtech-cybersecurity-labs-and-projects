# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 36: Risk Assessment

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **A.** Residual risk remains after existing controls are considered.
2. **A.** A useful scenario connects an enabling condition, event, and objective impact.
3. **A.** STRIDE systematically prompts threat categories.
4. **A.** Repudiation concerns denial of action and inadequate accountability.
5. **A.** Inherent risk represents exposure before controls.
6. **A.** Acceptance belongs to an authority accountable for business objectives.
7. **A.** Avoidance removes the activity or condition producing risk.
8. **A.** Risk decisions rely on imperfect data and assumptions.
9. **A.** Incidents and material changes invalidate prior assumptions.
10. **A.** Register review is a people-driven operational process.
