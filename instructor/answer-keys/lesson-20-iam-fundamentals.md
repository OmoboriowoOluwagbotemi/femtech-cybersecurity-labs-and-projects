# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 20: IAM Fundamentals

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **A.** RBAC associates permissions with roles and assigns identities to those roles.
2. **A.** Privilege creep is accumulated access that no longer matches current need.
3. **A.** A toxic combination creates fraud, abuse, or control-bypass risk through combined access.
4. **A.** JIT grants eligible privilege only for an approved time window.
5. **A.** Brokering can inject a secret without exposing it directly to the operator.
6. **A.** Mover processes recalculate access when responsibilities change.
7. **A.** Non-human identities often persist without clear owners, rotation, or interactive restrictions.
8. **A.** Emergency use requires immediate review, evidence preservation, and credential rotation where defined.
9. **A.** Reviewers need understandable context and proof that revoked access was actually removed.
10. **A.** Lifecycle execution is a people-driven operational procedure.
