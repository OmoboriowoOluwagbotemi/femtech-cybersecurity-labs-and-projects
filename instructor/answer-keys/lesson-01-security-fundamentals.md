# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 1: Security Fundamentals

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **C.** Unauthorized reading directly violates confidentiality.
2. **A.** A vulnerability is a weakness that a threat may exploit.
3. **B.** A SIEM alert identifies activity and therefore performs a detective function.
4. **C.** Layering reduces reliance on any one control and limits single-control failure.
5. **C.** Attack surface includes exposed technology, identities, services, cloud resources, and human interaction points.
6. **B.** Risk is the possibility that a threat exploits a vulnerability and causes harm.
7. **A.** Technical controls are implemented through hardware, software, or automation.
8. **C.** Directive controls tell people what behavior is required.
9. **B.** Integrity means data remains accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorized change.
10. **B.** Defense in depth combines multiple safeguards instead of depending on one control.

Short answer responses should demonstrate correct use of lesson terminology and connect security issues to business impact.
