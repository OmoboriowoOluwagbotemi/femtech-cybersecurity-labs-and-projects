# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 21: Endpoint Security

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **A.** EDR records and analyzes endpoint behavior and provides investigation and response capabilities.
2. **A.** Parent-child relationships reveal how execution began and progressed.
3. **A.** BitLocker reduces offline disclosure after a volume is locked.
4. **A.** Suspension retains encryption but temporarily changes protector enforcement for approved operations.
5. **A.** Recovery material can unlock data and is therefore a high-value secret.
6. **A.** Active exploitation, exposure, asset criticality, and impact create urgent enterprise risk.
7. **A.** Drift is the difference between actual state and approved baseline.
8. **A.** Excluded locations or processes may not receive normal inspection.
9. **A.** Isolation limits network paths but may not invalidate identity sessions or credentials.
10. **A.** Patch testing and rollout are people-driven operational processes.
