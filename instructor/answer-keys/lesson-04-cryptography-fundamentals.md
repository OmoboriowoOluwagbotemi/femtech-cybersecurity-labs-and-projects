# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 4: Cryptography Fundamentals

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **B.** Hashing produces a fixed-length one-way representation.
2. **A.** Symmetric encryption efficiently protects large data volumes.
3. **C.** The private key must remain under the exclusive control of its authorized holder.
4. **B.** A CA validates according to policy and signs certificates that bind identities to public keys.
5. **A.** Modern hostname validation uses the Subject Alternative Name extension.
6. **C.** TLS can provide confidentiality, integrity, and peer authentication for data in transit.
7. **B.** A unique salt prevents identical passwords from producing identical stored derivations.
8. **B.** Revocation invalidates a certificate before its scheduled expiration.
9. **A.** A verified digital signature supports integrity and authentication of the signing key.
10. **C.** Suspected private-key compromise requires revocation, replacement, investigation, and dependent-system review.

Short-answer and scenario responses should correctly use the terms key, digest, ciphertext, certificate, trust chain, revocation, data at rest, and data in transit.
