# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 29: Remediation and Reporting

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **A.** Remediation corrects or reduces the weakness and associated risk.
2. **A.** Mitigation reduces exploitability or impact while some weakness remains.
3. **A.** Enterprise priority requires threat and business context beyond technical severity.
4. **A.** Rings expose a controlled population before broad deployment.
5. **A.** Alternative controls require verified effectiveness and governed duration.
6. **A.** Residual risk must be accepted by an authority accountable for the business impact.
7. **A.** Reproducible technical evidence confirms that the weakness is corrected or controlled.
8. **A.** Absence can result from several conditions and requires evidence.
9. **A.** Executives need business impact, trends, blockers, accountability, and decisions.
10. **A.** Validation is a people-driven operational process.
