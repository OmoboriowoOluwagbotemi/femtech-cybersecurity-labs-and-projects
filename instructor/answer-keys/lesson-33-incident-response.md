# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 33: Incident Response

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **A.** An incident jeopardizes confidentiality, integrity, availability, or policy.
2. **A.** Revision 3 integrates IR recommendations across the CSF 2.0 functions.
3. **A.** Short-term containment acts quickly to limit ongoing damage.
4. **A.** Attackers may retain access through sessions, tokens, keys, consent, or persistence.
5. **A.** Eradication removes attacker mechanisms and corrects the enabling cause.
6. **A.** Recovery returns validated, monitored business service to a trusted state.
7. **A.** Chain of custody documents every evidence transfer and handling action.
8. **A.** Clear separation prevents assumptions from being communicated as facts.
9. **A.** Improvement actions require ownership, dates, and verification.
10. **A.** Coordinated containment is a people-driven operational process.
