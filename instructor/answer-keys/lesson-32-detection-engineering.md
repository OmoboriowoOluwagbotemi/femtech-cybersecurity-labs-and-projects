# Instructor Answer Key - Lesson 32: Detection Engineering

Do not distribute this file before the assessment closes. Accept defensible scenario and theory answers when students support their reasoning with accurate security concepts, evidence, and business context.

### Answer Key

1. **A.** A testable hypothesis defines expected behavior, evidence, and scope.
2. **A.** A data contract documents the source, fields, types, timing, and health on which logic depends.
3. **A.** Sigma expresses platform-neutral detection metadata and logic in structured form.
4. **A.** Conversion cannot guarantee correct field mapping, escaping, time, or backend semantics.
5. **A.** Mapping must reflect the technique directly observed by the rule.
6. **A.** A benign positive correctly matches conditions but represents authorized behavior.
7. **A.** End-to-end testing proves source creation through analyst-visible alert.
8. **A.** Tuning should remove specific benign causes while retaining suspicious variations.
9. **A.** Enrichment has lower business impact and builds automation confidence.
10. **A.** Peer review is a people-driven operational control.
